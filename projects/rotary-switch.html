Rotary Selector Switch & Mount

UWAFT

The design

A rotary selector switch and mount with a knob, bezel, housing and mounting plate for vehicle integration.

Design process

  1. Created a design document covering objectives, constraints, materials and manufacturing.
  2. Studied online and previous UWAFT mount implementations.
  3. Designed the assembly in Siemens NX using solid and surface modelling tools.

Results and skills

  • Reduced cost by 20 percent while improving safety, accessibility and durability.
  • Used a structured design process to optimize a practical, fully functional assembly.
  • Applied dimensioning and tolerancing for manufacture.
