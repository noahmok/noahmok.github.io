Solar Car Canopy

Oct 2026 · SolidWorks · Onboarding challenge, UW Midnight Sun

The design

A solar car canopy built entirely from surfaces and shaped to fit the challenge's dimensioning constraints while keeping airflow over it as clean as possible.

Design process

  1. Read the dimensioning constraints the canopy had to fit inside.
  2. Researched previous aerobody designs to see which shapes worked.
  3. Modelled the canopy with surface modeling in SolidWorks, refining the shape to minimize its drag area.

Results and skills

  • Reduced canopy drag area by more than 50 percent.
  • Surface modeling in SolidWorks, working to fixed dimensions.
  • Using past designs as research to guide a new one.

Computer Mouse

SolidWorks · Personal project

The design

A computer mouse modelled in SolidWorks with a sculpted shell made of separate surface patches, a scroll wheel and two side buttons.

Design process

  1. Shaped the shell as a set of surface patches, visible as the panels in the renders.
  2. Modelled the scroll wheel and side buttons as separate parts that sit in the shell.
  3. Checked the shape from the top and side views to keep the curves smooth.

Results and skills

  • Surface modeling: smooth transitions between neighbouring patches.
  • Fitting small functional parts into a curved body.
  • Presenting a model with clear, consistent views.

Rotary Selector Switch and Mount

Sep 2026 · Siemens NX · Onboarding challenge, UW Automobile Futures Team

The design

A rotary selector switch and its mount: a knob and bezel, a housing, and a mounting plate fastened with screws, designed for integration into a production vehicle.

Design process

  1. Examined the engineering drawing the part had to follow.
  2. Designed the switch and mount to meet its dimensioning, safety and manufacturing constraints.
  3. Refined the design for safety, accessibility and lifetime while keeping cost down.

Results and skills

  • Reduced cost by 20 percent while improving safety, accessibility and lifetime.
  • Siemens NX and reading engineering drawings.
  • Dimensioning and tolerancing with manufacturing in mind.

Publications

Isoperimetric sets of English words

Parabola · Volume 59, Issue 2 · 2023

Co-authored a mathematical investigation of isoperimetric sets in a space of the 3000 most common English words. The project used distance, boundaries, and perimeter to study word sets with minimum and maximum perimeter, with computation and Python used to generate and independently verify the results.

As part of the research, I led a team of 16 in investigating the mathematical structure of these word sets and how the results could help explain processes such as autocorrect.
