Solar Car Canopy

UWaterloo Midnight Sun

CAD Preview Drag to rotate · scroll to zoom

The Design

A canopy design that protects the driver and components while reducing drag within competition constraints.

Design Process

  1. Documented geometric and practical constraints of the car.
  2. Researched prior aerobody designs and canopy shapes to optimize drag performance.
  3. Created a surface model in SolidWorks using Boundary Surface, Knit Surface and Shell.

Results and Skills

  • Reduced canopy drag area by more than 50 percent.
  • Applied research and a complete design process.
  • Used complex surface modelling for aerodynamic simulations.
